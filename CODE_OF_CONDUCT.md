# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We want Brewake to be a welcoming, practical project where people can contribute regardless of experience, identity, background, or ability.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, patient, and specific.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Assume good intent while accepting corrections.
- Keep discussions relevant and avoid harassment, threats, discrimination, doxxing, or sexualized conduct.
- Protect private information and report security issues through the private process in `SECURITY.md`.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove content and restrict participation when behavior harms the project or its community. Enforcement decisions should consider context, impact, and repeated behavior.

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through GitHub. Reports will be reviewed as promptly and confidentially as practical. Good-faith reports will not result in retaliation.

This policy applies in repository spaces and when someone publicly represents the Brewake project.
